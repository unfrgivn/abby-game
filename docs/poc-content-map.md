# PoC content map

This document lists the PoC locations and the minimum interactive content required to satisfy `spec.md`.

## PoC locations

1. Hero’s House (interior)
2. School (interior)
3. Park / Playground (outdoor)
4. Arcade (interior)

## Traversal & scene links

- Main road connects House ↔ School ↔ Park ↔ Arcade
- At least one loop path to reduce backtracking
- Blocked paths to future areas are clearly marked (mountains, ocean, enchanted forest)

## Interactions by location

### Hero’s House
- Bed (respawn anchor; “Rest” optional)
- Journal access prompt (tutorial)
- Starter stickers granted on new game

### School
- 2+ blacklight notes
- 1 hidden door revealed by lantern
- 1 NPC who points the player toward using the lantern

### Park / Playground
- 1 visible Chaos Raccoon encounter
- 1 hidden door OR hidden note cluster (optional)
- 2–4 general hidden notes

### Arcade
- Claw machine mini-game
- Prize pool: gems + candy bars
- Optional hidden note in arcade interior

## Notes (minimum counts)

- Total hidden notes across PoC: 6–10
- School must have at least 2

## Doors (minimum counts)

- Total hidden doors across PoC: 2
- School must have at least 1

## Battles (PoC)

- Enemy family: Chaos Raccoon
- Encounter count: 1–2 (Park and/or near Arcade)
- Win rewards:
  - Gems (currency)
  - Sticker reward on first win: `Raccoon Dash`

## Sticker set (PoC minimum)

Starter (new game):
- `Bonk` (Attack)
- `Glitter Bandage` (Support)
- `Pocket Sand` (Utility)

First battle reward:
- `Raccoon Dash` (Attack/Utility)
